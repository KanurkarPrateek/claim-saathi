His room cost ₹5,000/day; the policy allowed ₹3,000. So the insurer also paid doctor, nursing and procedure charges at only 60% — the proportionate deduction, buried in clause 3.1(a). Nobody told him at the admission desk.

Claim Saathi would have shown him, before admission, that a twin-sharing room costs him ₹17,200 less.
